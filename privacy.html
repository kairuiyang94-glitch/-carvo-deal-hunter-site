Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

CARVO Deal Hunter is a personal automation tool operated in Sydney, Australia. Contact: kairuiyang94@gmail.com.

Information accessed

When authorized by the Gmail account holder, the tool accesses Gmail messages relevant to Carsales vehicle alerts. It reads message content and metadata needed to identify listing details such as model, advertised price, odometer and listing URL. It may use Gmail labels to track alerts already processed.

Purposes and processing

Information is used to screen vehicle listings and send selected listing summaries to the operator’s private Telegram chat. If optional AI analysis is enabled, extracted listing information and relevant alert text may be sent to OpenAI for automated risk commentary. Telegram receives selected alert summaries. These providers process data under their respective terms and policies.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials and integration tokens are intended to be stored as private GitHub Actions secrets, not in this public website. The workflow does not intentionally maintain a separate persistent database of complete Gmail messages; Gmail retains the source email and processing labels. Third-party services may retain transmitted content according to their policies. No security method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Sharing and sale

Gmail information is used to run this operator-controlled workflow, not sold or used for advertising. It is disclosed to the configured Telegram and optional AI providers only as needed for the described processing.

Retention and deletion

Source messages and processing labels remain in the Gmail account until removed by the account holder. Telegram summaries may remain in the destination chat until deleted there. You can revoke app access through your Google Account’s connected-app settings and ask the operator to stop processing or delete data under their control using the contact email above. Third-party retention is governed by each provider.

Google API data

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs are limited to providing the described alert-processing functionality and will follow applicable Google API Services User Data Policy requirements, including Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Changes

This policy may be updated when data practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page.
