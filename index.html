CARVO Deal Hunter

A personal vehicle-listing alert analysis tool. CARVO Deal Hunter helps its operator review Carsales saved-search email notifications and identify listings worth further inspection.

What it does

With the operator’s permission, it reads relevant Gmail notifications from Carsales, extracts advertised vehicle details, applies preliminary screening criteria, and sends selected summaries to a private Telegram chat. An optional AI-assisted analysis produces general risk notes.

Important limitations

Vehicle scores are preliminary, not verified valuations or guarantees of condition, ownership, availability, or profit. Listings must be independently checked before any purchase decision.

Access and privacy

This is an operator-controlled tool and not a public vehicle marketplace. For information on Gmail access, data use, retention and deletion, see our Privacy Policy.
